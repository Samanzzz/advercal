Your menu and hours, readable on a phone

Menus, hours, the address, services and prices go on the page as real text. They read well on a small screen, and search engines can read them too.

If you already take bookings or orders through OpenTable, Resy, Toast, Square or Calendly, the site links straight to it with a clear button. We don't build a booking system of our own, so the way you take bookings stays as it is.

A report on the first of every month

On monthly care, we check your site on the first of the month and email you a short report in plain language, with a PDF. It covers security and your domain, how the site looks on six screen sizes, accessibility, speed on a phone, search titles and broken links.

We fix what the check finds and make small text and photo updates when you ask.
