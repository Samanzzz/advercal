Questions we hear

What does a site cost?

It depends on how many pages you need and what they have to do. Tell us about your project and we will send a written quote before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Most sites take about a week once we have your words and photos. A bigger site, or one that needs writing from scratch, takes longer, and we tell you the date up front.

Who owns the site?

You do. We publish it on your own domain and, wherever we can, under your own account, so it keeps running even if you stop working with us.

Can people book with me through the site?

Yes. If you already use a booking service such as OpenTable, Square or Calendly, we add a clear button that takes visitors straight to it.

Can you redo the site I already have?

Yes. We start from what is there, keep what works, and rebuild the rest. Your address stays the same.

What happens after launch?

With monthly care we check the site on the first of every month, fix what we find and send you the report. Without it, you can still email us whenever you need a change.
