From the first email to launch

  1. We learn about the business

    A short questionnaire, and a call if you want one. Send us whatever already exists: an old site, a menu or price list, photos, your listings.

  2. We write and design the site

    We write the words from what you send and design the pages around them.

  3. You get a private preview

    A link to the whole finished site, to open on your phone or your computer.

  4. We make your changes

    Tell us what to change in your own words. A reply to our email is enough.

  5. It launches on your domain

    When you say it's ready, it goes live at your own address.

Two of us look at every change

The person you talk to builds your site, and a second partner checks every change before it goes out.

Every page also passes our security check before it goes live. Publishing stays blocked while the check finds anything high-risk. The pages are plain, with no page builder and no plugins to keep patched.

The site and the domain are yours

You own the site and the domain. Wherever we can, we publish it under your own account, so it keeps running if you ever stop working with us.
