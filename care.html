The check

  1. Security and HTTPS

    The certificate and the domain, including when each one expires.

  2. Six screen sizes

    How every page looks, from a small phone to a large monitor.

  3. Accessibility

    Problems that make the site hard to read or use for people with a disability.

  4. Speed on a phone

    How long the site takes to load, measured on a phone.

  5. Search and sharing

    The titles search engines show, and the preview people see when someone sends them a link.

  6. Links and details

    Broken links and details that have gone out of date, like last year’s copyright.

Fixes and small updates
